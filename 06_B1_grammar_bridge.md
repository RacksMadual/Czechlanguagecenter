# B1 Grammar Bridge — From A2 to B1
## What's New at B1 That You Haven't Learned Yet

> **Your situation:** You know A2 grammar well (nominative, accusative, present/past/future tense,
> basic conjugations, gender of nouns). The B1 citizenship exam requires all of those PLUS the
> topics in this file.
>
> **Strategy:** Learn these topics in the order given. Each builds on what came before.
> Work through this with Petra in your lessons.

---

## The B1 Exam vs. Your A2 Knowledge

| Feature | A2 (what you know) | B1 (what you need to add) |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Cases | Nominative + Accusative | + Dative, Locative, Instrumental, Genitive plural |
| Tenses | Present, Past, Future | Same + understanding aspect fully |
| Verbs | Basic conjugations | + Imperatives, Conditionals, Passive, Aspect pairs |
| Writing | 40–60 words | 100–120 words, discursive topics |
| Speaking | Individual, 15 min | **In pairs**, 15–18 min, more complex tasks |
| Texts | Simple | More complex articles, authentic texts |

## NEW TOPIC 1: The Case System — Full Picture

You know Case 1 (Nominative) and Case 4 (Accusative). Here are all 7:

| Case | Czech | Question | Main use |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| **1. Nominativ** | nominativ | Kdo? Co? | Subject |
| **2. Genitiv** | genitiv | Koho? Čeho? | Possession, negation, "of" |
| **3. Dativ** | dativ | Komu? Čemu? | Indirect object, "to/for whom" |
| **4. Akuzativ** | akuzativ | Koho? Co? | Direct object (you know this!) |
| **5. Vokativ** | vokativ | — | Addressing people |
| **6. Lokativ** | lokativ | O kom? O čem? | Location "in/on/about" |
| **7. Instrumentál** | instrumentál | S kým? S čím? | With whom/what, means |

## NEW TOPIC 2: Case 3 — Dative (Dativ)

**Question: Komu? Čemu?** (To whom? For what?)

**Main uses:**
- Indirect object (who receives the action)
- After certain prepositions: **k/ke** (to, towards), **kvůli** (because of), **díky** (thanks to)
- Fixed expressions: **líbit se**, **chutnat**, **být + age**, **pomáhat**

### Dative singular endings:

| Gender | Nominative (base) | Dative |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| Masculine animate | student, kamarád | student**ovi**, kamarád**ovi** |
| Masculine inanimate | hotel, stůl | hotel**u**, stol**u** |
| Feminine (-a) | sestra, žena | sestr**e**, žen**ě** |
| Feminine (-e/-ice) | práce, nemocnice | prác**i**, nemocnic**i** |
| Neuter | město, moře | měst**u**, moř**i** |

### Key verbs that use dative:

| Czech | English | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| dát (někomu) | to give (to someone) | Dám **Petrovi** knihu. |
| říct (někomu) | to tell (someone) | Řekl jsem **učitelce**. |
| pomoci (někomu) | to help (someone) | Pomáhám **kamarádovi**. |
| líbit se (komu) | to like / to appeal to | **Mně** se líbí Praha. = I like Prague. |
| chutnat (komu) | to taste good to | **Mi** chutná guláš. = I like goulash. |
| být (komu) let | to be (someone's) age | **Je mi** 35 let. (you already know this!) |
| patřit (komu) | to belong to | Tato kniha patří **mně**. |
| telefonovat (komu) | to call (someone) | Telefonoval jsem **manažerovi**. |

### Dative personal pronouns:

| Person | Nominative | Dative (long) | Dative (short) |
|--------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| já | já | mně | mi |
| ty | ty | tobě | ti |
| on | on | jemu | mu |
| ona | ona | jí | jí |
| my | my | nám | nám |
| vy | vy | vám | vám |
| oni | oni | jim | jim |

> **Tip:** Short forms (mi, ti, mu, etc.) go **after the first word** in a sentence.
> Mně/tobě etc. go at the start/end or after prepositions.
>
> Líbí **se mi** Praha. *(I like Prague.)*
> Dal jsem **mu** klíče. *(I gave him the keys.)*

## NEW TOPIC 3: Case 6 — Locative (Lokativ)

**Question: O kom? O čem? Kde?** (About whom/what? Where?)

**Always used WITH a preposition:**
- **v/ve** (in) — Jsem **v Praze**.
- **na** (on/at) — Pracuju **na letišti**.
- **o** (about) — Mluvíme **o práci**.
- **při** (during/while) — **Při práci** poslouchám hudbu.
- **po** (after/around) — Procházím se **po Praze**.

### Locative singular endings:

| Gender | Nominative | Locative | Preposition example |
|--------|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| Masculine | hotel, Praha, Petr | hotel**u**, Praze, Petrov**i** | v hotelu, v Praze, o Petrovi |
| Masculine (-k/-h/-ch) | obchod, jazyk | obchod**ě**, jazyk**u** | v obchodě, v jazyce |
| Feminine (-a) | škola, Praha | škol**e**, Praze | ve škole, v Praze |
| Feminine (-e/-ice) | práce, nemocnice | prác**i**, nemocnic**i** | v práci, v nemocnici |
| Neuter (-o) | město | měst**ě** | ve městě |
| Neuter (-í) | nádraží | nádraží | na nádraží |

**You already use locative!** These are forms you've heard in lessons:
- v Praze *(in Prague)*
- v kanceláři *(in the office)*
- doma *(at home)*
- v práci *(at work)*
- v restauraci *(in the restaurant)*
- v nemocnici *(in the hospital)*

Now you know WHY they change!

## NEW TOPIC 4: Case 7 — Instrumental (Instrumentál)

**Question: S kým? S čím? Čím?** (With whom? With what? By what means?)

**Main uses:**
- With **s/se** = with someone/something
- Transport / means: jet **autobusem**, jet **metrem** (you already use this!)
- Profession after **být**: Jsem **účetním**. *(I am an accountant.)*
- After **stát se** (to become): Stal jsem se **manažerem**.

### Instrumental singular endings:

| Gender | Nominative | Instrumental |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Masculine animate | student, muž | student**em**, muž**em** |
| Masculine inanimate | autobus, vlak | autobus**em**, vlak**em** |
| Feminine (-a) | žena, sestra | žen**ou**, sestr**ou** |
| Feminine (-e) | práce, sklenice | prací, sklenicí |
| Neuter (-o) | město, auto | měst**em**, aut**em** |

**Instrumental personal pronouns:**

| Person | Instrumental |
|--------|-------------|
| já | mnou |
| ty | tebou |
| on | ním |
| ona | ní |
| my | námi |
| vy | vámi |
| oni | nimi |

**Examples:**
- Jedu **metrem**. *(I go by metro.)* — transport
- Jdu **s kamarádem**. *(I go with a friend.)* — with
- Pracuji **jako manažer** → jsem **manažerem**. *(I am a manager.)* — profession
- Zaplatil jsem **kartou**. *(I paid by card.)* — means

## NEW TOPIC 5: Case 2 — Genitive (Genitiv) — Expanded

You've met genitive with **vedle** and dates. Now the full picture:

**Question: Koho? Čeho?**

**Main uses:**
- Possession: kniha **kamaráda** *(friend's book)*
- After negation: Nemám **čas**. → Nemám **čas** (acc.) vs. Nemám **žádného** přítele (gen.)
- Quantity: hodně **práce**, málo **peněz**, litr **vody**
- After prepositions: **z/ze** (from), **do** (to/into), **od** (from), **bez** (without), **u** (at someone's place), **vedle** (next to), **blízko** (near), **kvůli** (because of), **během** (during), **kromě** (except for)

### Genitive singular endings:

| Gender | Nominative | Genitive |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Masculine animate | student, kamarád | student**a**, kamarád**a** |
| Masculine inanimate | hotel, stůl | hotel**u**, stolu |
| Feminine (-a) | žena, Praha | žen**y**, Prah**y** |
| Feminine (-e) | práce, nemocnice | prác**e**, nemocnic**e** |
| Neuter (-o) | město, auto | měst**a**, aut**a** |
| Neuter (-í) | nádraží | nádraží |

**Examples:**
- Jsem **z České republiky**. *(I am from Czech Republic.)*
- Jdu **do práce**. *(I go to work.)*
- Přišel jsem **od kamaráda**. *(I came from a friend's place.)*
- Mám hodně **práce**. *(I have a lot of work.)*
- Sklenice **vody**, prosím. *(A glass of water, please.)*
- Přijdu **bez auta**. *(I'll come without a car.)*

## NEW TOPIC 6: Plural Forms

At B1, you must use all cases in **plural** too. This is the hardest part.

### Plural nominative (subject plural) — basics:

| Gender | Singular | Plural | Notes |
|--------|---------|--------|-------|
| Masculine animate | student | student**i** | -i ending |
| Masculine inanimate | hotel | hotel**y** | -y ending |
| Feminine (-a) | žena | žen**y** | -y ending |
| Feminine (-e) | práce | prác**e** | unchanged |
| Neuter (-o) | město | měst**a** | -a ending |
| Neuter (-í) | nádraží | nádraží | unchanged |

### Genitive plural (most complex — learn key words):

| Gender | Example | Genitive plural |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Masculine animate | student | student**ů** |
| Masculine inanimate | hotel | hotel**ů** |
| Feminine (-a) | žena | **žen** (zero ending!) |
| Feminine (-e) | práce | prací |
| Neuter (-o) | město | **měst** (zero ending!) |
| Neuter (-í) | nádraží | nádraží |

**Key genitive plural patterns to memorize:**
- Kolik **studentů**? *(How many students?)*
- Hodně **žen**. *(Many women.)*
- Pět **měst**. *(Five cities.)*
- Deset **korun**. *(Ten crowns.)*
- Týden **hodin** češtiny. *(A week of Czech lessons.)*

### Special declensions you must learn:
| Word | Singular | Plural (nominative) |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| den (day) | den | dny / dní (gen. pl.) |
| týden (week) | týden | týdny / týdnů (gen. pl.) |
| rok (year) | rok | roky / let (gen. pl. = let!) |
| člověk (person) | člověk | **lidé** (completely different!) |
| dítě (child) | dítě | **děti** |

> **Critical:** 5 dní (5 days), 5 týdnů (5 weeks), 5 let (5 years — NOT roků!), 5 lidí (5 people)

## NEW TOPIC 7: Verbal Aspect — Perfective vs. Imperfective

This is the most important B1 grammar concept. Czech verbs come in **pairs**:

| Imperfective (nedokonavý) | Perfective (dokonavý) | English |
|--------------------------|----------------------|---------|
| psát | na**psát** | to write |
| číst | pře**číst** | to read |
| dělat | u**dělat** | to do/make |
| kupovat | koupit | to buy |
| vařit | u**vařit** | to cook |
| telefonovat | za**telefonovat** | to call |
| jíst | s**níst** | to eat |
| pít | vy**pít** | to drink |
| říkat | říct/říci | to say |
| chodit | jít | to go (on foot, once) |
| jezdit | jet | to go (by vehicle, once) |
| volat | za**volat** | to call |
| učit se | na**učit se** | to learn |
| pracovat | od**pracovat** | to work (through) |

### The key rule:

| Use | Verb type | Example |
|-----|----------|---------|
| Ongoing / repeated action | **Imperfective** | Každý den **píšu** emaily. *(I write emails every day.)* |
| Completed, single action | **Perfective** | Včera jsem **napsal** email. *(Yesterday I wrote/finished an email.)* |
| Present tense | **Imperfective only** | Teď **píšu** email. *(I'm writing an email now.)* |
| Future completed action | **Perfective** | Zítra **napíšu** email. *(Tomorrow I will write the email — and finish it.)* |
| Future ongoing | **Budu** + imperfective | Zítra **budu psát** celý den. *(Tomorrow I'll be writing all day.)* |

**In past tense — the meaning shifts:**
- **Psal** jsem email. *(I was writing an email — process, maybe not finished)*
- **Napsal** jsem email. *(I wrote an email — finished, done)*

> **Memory tip:** If you can add "all the way" or "and I finished it" → use perfective.
> If the action was habitual, ongoing, or in progress → use imperfective.

## NEW TOPIC 8: Imperative — Rozkazovací způsob

Used when giving instructions, orders, or making requests.

**Formation from the 3rd person singular (on/ona form):**

| Verb | on/ona form | Imperative (ty) | Imperative (vy) |
|------|------------|----------------|----------------|
| dělat | dělá | Dělej! | Dělejte! |
| číst | čte | Čti! | Čtěte! |
| psát | píše | Piš! | Pište! |
| jít | jde | Jdi! | Jděte! |
| jíst | jí | Jez! | Jezte! |
| mluvit | mluví | Mluv! | Mluvte! |
| pracovat | pracuje | Pracuj! | Pracujte! |
| být | je | Buď! | Buďte! |
| počkat | počká | Počkej! | Počkejte! |

**With negation — use imperfective:**
- Nedělej to! *(Don't do that!)*
- Nemluv tak rychle! *(Don't speak so fast!)*

**Common exam imperatives:**
| Czech | English |
|-------|---------|
| Poslyšte! | Listen! (formal) |
| Podívejte se! | Look! (formal) |
| Promiňte! | Excuse me! / Sorry! |
| Počkejte! | Wait! |
| Pomozte mi! | Help me! |
| Zavolejte záchranku! | Call an ambulance! |
| Jděte rovně! | Go straight! |
| Otočte se! | Turn around! |

## NEW TOPIC 9: Conditional — Podmiňovací způsob

Used for wishes, polite requests, hypothetical situations.

**Formation: past participle + by (conditional particle)**

| Person | By-form |
|--------|---------|
| já | bych |
| ty | bys |
| on/ona | by |
| my | bychom |
| vy | byste |
| oni | by |

**Examples:**
- Chtěl **bych** kávu. *(I would like a coffee.)* — VERY useful for ordering!
- Mohl **bych** mluvit s manažerem? *(Could I speak with the manager?)*
- Rád **bych** studoval češtinu. *(I would like to study Czech.)*
- **Kdybych** měl více času, studoval bych více. *(If I had more time, I would study more.)*
- **Aby**ste rozuměli, musíte poslouchat. *(In order for you to understand, you must listen.)*

**Key conditional phrases (polite register):**
| Czech | English |
|-------|---------|
| Chtěl/a bych... | I would like... |
| Mohl/a bych...? | Could I...? |
| Mohli byste...? | Could you...? (formal) |
| Rád/ráda bych... | I would like to... |
| Kdybych mohl/a... | If I could... |
| Co byste doporučil/a? | What would you recommend? |
| Byl/a byste tak laskav/á...? | Would you be so kind as to...? |

## NEW TOPIC 10: Passive Voice — Trpný rod

Used when you don't say WHO does the action (focus on what happens to the object).

**Formation: být + past participle (passive)**

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Petra napsal email. | Email **byl napsán**. *(The email was written.)* |
| Vláda podepsala zákon. | Zákon **byl podepsán** vládou. *(The law was signed by the government.)* |

**More common in Czech: reflexive passive with SE:**
- Tady **se mluví** česky. *(Czech is spoken here.)*
- V ČR **se říká**... *(In Czech Republic it is said/people say...)*
- **Prodává se** dům. *(House for sale — literally "is being sold")*
- **Otevírá se** v 9 hodin. *(It opens at 9 o'clock.)*

## NEW TOPIC 11: Comparison of Adjectives

**Comparative (more ___): add -ější or -ejší**

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| dobrý (good) | lepší | nejlepší |
| špatný (bad) | horší | nejhorší |
| velký (big) | větší | největší |
| malý (small) | menší | nejmenší |
| starý (old) | starší | nejstarší |
| mladý (young) | mladší | nejmladší |
| drahý (expensive) | dražší | nejdražší |
| levný (cheap) | levnější | nejlevnější |
| hezký (nice) | hezčí | nejhezčí |
| rychlý (fast) | rychlejší | nejrychlejší |
| pomalý (slow) | pomalejší | nejpomalejší |

**Using comparatives:**
- Praha je **větší než** Brno. *(Prague is bigger than Brno.)*
- Tato restaurace je **lepší než** ta druhá. *(This restaurant is better than the other one.)*
- Mluvte **pomaleji**, prosím! *(Speak more slowly, please!)*
- To je **nejlepší** film, co jsem viděl. *(That's the best film I've seen.)*

## NEW TOPIC 12: Reflexive Verbs — Se/Si

You already know **jmenovat se, líbit se, dívat se**. At B1, you need to use se/si more precisely.

**SE = general reflexive (the action bounces back to subject)**
- Mýt **se** — to wash oneself
- Oblékat **se** — to get dressed
- Učit **se** — to learn / study
- Cítit **se** — to feel
- Sejít **se** — to meet (each other)

**SI = reflexive with dative meaning (for oneself)**
- Koupit **si** — to buy for oneself → Koupil jsem **si** nové auto. *(I bought myself a new car.)*
- Dát **si** — to have (food/drink) → Dám **si** kávu. *(I'll have a coffee.)* — you know this!
- Přát **si** — to wish for → Přeji **si** mluvit s manažerem. *(I wish to speak with the manager.)*
- Vzít **si** — to take for oneself → Vezmu **si** čas. *(I'll take time.)*

## NEW TOPIC 13: Conjunctions at B1 Level

These connect ideas and are essential for writing 100-120 word texts coherently:

| Czech | English | Type |
|-------|---------|------|
| **protože** | because | cause |
| **aby** | so that / in order to | purpose |
| **když** | when / if (present) | time/condition |
| **pokud** | if (formal) | condition |
| **kdybych/kdyby** | if (conditional) | hypothetical |
| **přestože / i když** | even though / although | contrast |
| **zatímco** | while / whereas | contrast |
| **jak...tak** | both...and | addition |
| **než** | than / before | comparison / time |
| **dokud** | until / as long as | time |
| **jakmile** | as soon as | time |
| **proto** | therefore / that's why | result |
| **tedy / takže** | so / therefore | result |
| **totiž** | you see / namely | explanation |
| **navíc** | moreover / in addition | addition |
| **naopak** | on the contrary | contrast |
| **například** | for example | example |

**Using aby (so that / in order to):**
- Studuji češtinu, **aby**ch získal občanství. *(I study Czech in order to get citizenship.)*
- Mluvte pomaleji, **aby**ch rozuměl. *(Speak slower so that I understand.)*

**Using přestože (even though):**
- **Přestože** je čeština těžká, studuji každý den. *(Even though Czech is hard, I study every day.)*

## NEW TOPIC 14: Numbers and Their Declension

At B1, numbers must agree with nouns in case. Key patterns:

**1–4: adjective-like agreement**
- jeden muž / jedna žena / jedno dítě

**5+: govern genitive plural**
- pět **mužů**, deset **žen**, sto **dětí**

**Special: age, years, time periods**
- Je mi **35 let**. *(I am 35 years old.)* — let = genitive plural of rok
- Bydlím tady **9 let**. *(I've lived here 9 years.)*
- Za **5 minut**. *(In 5 minutes.)* — minut = genitive plural

## Summary: What to Study in What Order

**Priority 1 (Start immediately — most common in exam):**
1. Locative singular — you already use it, now understand the rules
2. Dative singular — mně, ti, mu, jí, nám, vám, jim (short forms)
3. Conditional — Chtěl bych... / Mohl bych...?
4. Verbal aspect basics — completed vs. ongoing

**Priority 2 (Next 1–2 months):**
5. Instrumental singular — s kamarádem, metrem, autem
6. Genitive uses — z, do, od, bez, u, vedle + singular
7. Imperative — giving instructions clearly
8. Comparison of adjectives — víc/méně, lepší/horší

**Priority 3 (Advanced — 3–5 months):**
9. Plural cases (nominative, genitive, dative, locative, instrumental)
10. Passive voice with se
11. Special plurals: lidé, děti, let, dní
12. Advanced conjunctions: přestože, jakmile, zatímco
