**Focus: Make your A2 knowledge automatic before moving to B1.**

## Phase 2: Bridge to B1 (Months 3–6 with Petra)

**Focus: Learn the new B1 grammar step by step (File 06).**

### Suggested order for lessons with Petra:

**Month 3:**
- Locative singular — why v Praze, v práci, v nemocnici change (already in your vocab!)
- Dative singular — mi, ti, mu, jí, nám, vám, jim (short forms first)
- Key dative verbs: líbit se, chutnat, dát, říct, pomoci

**Month 4:**
- Instrumental singular — with s (kamarádem), transport (metrem, autem)
- Full genitive singular — after z, do, od, bez, u, vedle
- Comparative adjectives — lepší, horší, větší, menší

**Month 5:**
- Conditional — Chtěl bych, Mohl bych, Kdybych
- Imperative — commands and polite requests
- Verbal aspect basics — perfective vs. imperfective pairs

**Month 6:**
- Plural nominative + genitive plural (key words: studentů, žen, měst, let, lidí, dětí)
- Passive with se — tady se mluví česky
- Advanced conjunctions: aby, přestože, zatímco, jakmile

## Phase 3: B1 Consolidation (Months 6–8)

**Focus: Practice exam-style tasks, build writing fluency.**

### Weekly practice routine:

| Day | Activity | Time |
|-----|---------|------|
| Monday | Read one Czech article (from iDnes.cz or Czech Radio) | 20 min |
| Tuesday | Write one 100-word text on a B1 topic | 30 min |
| Wednesday | Listen to Czech Radio (ČRo) news | 15 min |
| Thursday | Vocabulary review (new words from reading) | 20 min |
| Friday | Czech lesson with Petra | 60 min |
| Weekend | Watch Czech TV (ČT1) with subtitles | 30 min |

### Writing practice targets (each week write one):
- Week 1: Formal email (accommodation inquiry)
- Week 2: Informal email (to a friend)
- Week 3: Opinion text (advantages/disadvantages)
- Week 4: Description of a past experience

### Mock exam: 2 weeks before your real exam
- Do a complete timed practice test (all 4 sections)
- Review your mistakes with Petra

## The Life & Institutions Exam — Quick Action Plan

> **This exam can be taken SEPARATELY from the language exam.**
> You can take it sooner because it tests knowledge, not language level.
> All 300 possible questions are published online!

**6-week plan:**
- Week 1: Topics 1–5 (State symbols, holidays, government, president, legal system)
- Week 2: Topics 6–10 (Health, social security, employment, education, police)
- Week 3: Topics 11–16 (Local government, EU, rights, housing, consumer, traditions)
- Week 4: Topics 17–23 (Geography — neighboring countries, regions, rivers, mountains, places)
- Week 5: Topics 24–30 (History, heroes, culture, inventions, sports, literature)
- Week 6: Full mock test + review weak areas

**Download the full 300-question database from:**
https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/obcanstvi/en/databanka-uloh/
