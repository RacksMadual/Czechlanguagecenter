### Topic 1: State Symbols — Státní symboly

**The flag (vlajka):**
- Czech flag = white, red, and blue triangle
- White = top stripe, Red = bottom stripe, Blue = triangle on left

**The coat of arms (státní znak):**
- Has three parts: silver (white) two-tailed lion on red = Bohemia (Čechy)
- Black eagle = Silesia (Slezsko)
- Red-white eagle = Moravia (Morava)

**The national anthem (státní hymna):**
- Called "Kde domov můj?" (Where is my home?)
- First line = Kde domov můj? (Where is my home?)
- Written by Josef Kajetán Tyl (1834)

### Topic 3: Government Structure — Státní správa

**Czech Republic is a parliamentary democracy (parlamentní demokracie)**

**Parliament = Parlament**
The Czech Parliament has **TWO chambers (dvě komory):**

**1. Chamber of Deputies = Poslanecká sněmovna**
- 200 elected members (200 poslanců)
- Elected every **4 years**
- Makes laws

**2. Senate = Senát**
- **81 senators** (81 senátorů) — REMEMBER THIS NUMBER!
- Elected every 6 years (1/3 every 2 years)
- Reviews laws from Chamber of Deputies

**President = Prezident**
- Elected directly by citizens every **5 years** (since 2012)
- Maximum 2 terms
- Current president (2023–): **Petr Pavel**
- Previous president: **Miloš Zeman** (2013-2023)

**Government = Vláda**
- Led by Prime Minister (Předseda vlády / Premiér)
- The government is responsible to the Chamber of Deputies

### Topic 6: Health System — Zdravotní systém **[IMPORTANT]**

**Key facts:**
- Health care in Czech Republic is largely **free** for insured people
- Every employed person must have **zdravotní pojištění** (health insurance)
- Employer pays most of the insurance; employee pays a smaller part
- For **unemployed people registered at the labour office (úřad práce)**, the **state pays health insurance**
- Emergency number: **155** (záchranná služba / ambulance)
- Police: **158**
- European emergency: **112**

### Topic 16: Czech Customs & Traditions — Zvyky a tradice **[IMPORTANT]**

**Major Czech traditions:**

| Czech name | English | When |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Vánoce | Christmas | 24-26 December |
| Velikonoce | Easter | Spring (variable) |
| Mikuláš | St. Nicholas Day | 5-6 December |

**Christmas (Vánoce):**
- Celebrated on **24 December** (Štědrý den = Christmas Eve)
- Traditional food: **kapr** (carp) + potato salad
- Gifts brought by **Ježíšek** (Baby Jesus) — NOT Santa!

**Easter (Velikonoce):**
- Boys/men gently hit girls with willow branches (pomlázka)
- Girls give decorated eggs (kraslice) and chocolate

**Dušičky (All Souls' Day — 2 November):**
- Czech tradition: decorate graves with **flowers**, light candles, remember deceased

**Mikuláš (5-6 December):**
- St. Nicholas comes with angel (anděl) and devil (čert)
- Good children get sweets, naughty ones get coal

### Topic 24: Czech National Heroes — Národní hrdinové **[IMPORTANT]**

**Cyril and Methodius (Cyril a Metoděj):**
- Byzantine missionaries, came to Great Moravia in **863 AD**
- Created the **Glagolitic alphabet** to write Slavic languages
- Translated Bible into Old Slavic
- Their feast day: **5 July** (state holiday!)

**Prince Wenceslas (Kníže Václav):**
- Přemyslid prince, 10th century
- Patron saint of Bohemia
- Murdered by his brother Boleslav
- **Czech Statehood Day = 28 September** (his feast day)
- The famous painting is on Wenceslas Square (Václavské náměstí) in Prague

**Jan Hus:**
- Czech religious reformer, 15th century
- Professor at Prague university (Karlova univerzita)
- Preached against corruption in the Catholic Church
- Burned at the stake in Konstanz (Kostnice) on **6 July 1415**
- State holiday = **6 July** (Den upálení mistra Jana Husa)
- Inspired the Hussite Wars

**Jan Amos Komenský (Comenius):**
- 17th century educator
- Called "teacher of nations"
- Wrote first illustrated children's textbook
- Pioneer of modern education methods

### Topic 30: Czech Literature & Education

**The first Czech university:**
- **Karlova univerzita** (Charles University) in Prague
- Founded in **1348** by King Karel IV (Charles IV)
- One of oldest universities in Central Europe

**Jan Hus** was a professor at Charles University — connects to Topic 24.

**Czech National Revival (Národní obrození):**
- Late 18th — early 19th century
- Effort to revive Czech language and culture after German dominance
- Key figures: Josef Dobrovský (linguist), Josef Jungmann (dictionary), František Palacký (historian)

**Key fact:** Karel IV (Charles IV) — Holy Roman Emperor and Czech king — built:
- Charles University (1348)
- Charles Bridge (started 1357)
- St. Vitus Cathedral (Prague Castle)
- New Town of Prague (Nové Město)
