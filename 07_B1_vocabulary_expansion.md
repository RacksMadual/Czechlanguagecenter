# B1 Vocabulary Expansion
## Deeper Vocabulary for the Citizenship Language Exam

> At B1, you need richer vocabulary on the same topics as A2, plus the ability to
> express opinions, describe experiences, and discuss abstract ideas.
> This file expands each exam topic beyond what you know at A2.

## Key B1 Linking Words for Writing

These are essential for writing coherent texts of 100–120 words:

**Adding information:**
- Navíc, ... *(Moreover,...)*
- Kromě toho, ... *(In addition,...)*
- Také / Taktéž... *(Also...)*
- A také... *(And also...)*

**Contrasting:**
- Avšak / Nicméně... *(However,...)*
- Na druhou stranu... *(On the other hand,...)*
- Přestože / Ačkoli... *(Although / Even though...)*
- Naopak... *(On the contrary...)*
- Přesto... *(Nevertheless / Still...)*

**Giving reasons:**
- Protože... *(Because...)*
- Vzhledem k tomu, že... *(Given that...)*
- Z důvodu... *(Due to...)*
- Díky tomu, že... *(Thanks to the fact that...)*

**Drawing conclusions:**
- Proto... *(Therefore...)*
- Takže... *(So therefore...)*
- Z toho vyplývá, že... *(From this it follows that...)*
- Celkově vzato... *(Overall...)*
- Závěrem lze říci, že... *(In conclusion, it can be said that...)*

**Ordering ideas:**
- Za prvé... za druhé... za třetí... *(Firstly... secondly... thirdly...)*
- Nejprve... Potom... Nakonec... *(First... Then... Finally...)*
- Na jedné straně... Na druhé straně... *(On one hand... On the other hand...)*
