## Your TWO Citizenship Exams

### Exam 1: Czech Language Exam — **B1 Level**
Organised by ÚJOP Charles University · Fee: **CZK 3,700**

| Section | Time | Pass mark |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Reading comprehension | 50 min | 60% |
| Listening comprehension | 30–35 min | 60% (or 50% if it's your one allowed lower section) |
| Writing task (100–120 words) | 60 min | 60% |
| Speaking (in pairs) | 15–18 min | 60% |

- You must reach **60% in every section** to pass (50% is allowed in **one** written section)
- Results published ~30 days after exam; certificate issued within 7 weeks
- No limit on retakes; register **at least 14 days** before your chosen date

> **Note:** The A2 exam is for *permanent residence* (trvalý pobyt). The citizenship exam is B1.
> If you haven't yet passed A2, that comes first — your B1 materials are files 06–08.

### Exam 2: Czech Life & Institutions Exam
- 30 multiple-choice questions · 30 minutes
- Need **18/30 correct (60%)** to pass
- All 300 possible questions are published online — you can study them all!
- Can be taken **independently** from the language exam — do this one first!
