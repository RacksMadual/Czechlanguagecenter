# Czech Learning Materials — Prince's Study Guide
## For A1/A2 Level → Czech Citizenship Exam

This folder contains adapted study materials based on your lessons with Petra,
combined with everything needed for the Czech citizenship exam.

---

## Your Exam Has TWO Parts

### Part 1: Czech Language Exam (A2 Level)
**Written** (105 min total) + **Speaking** (15–20 min)
- Reading → 25 points
- Writing → 20 points
- Listening → 25 points
- Speaking → 40 points
- **You must score at least 60% in EACH section to pass**

### Part 2: Czech Life & Institutions Exam
- 30 multiple-choice questions (4 options, 1 correct)
- 30 minutes
- Need **18/30 correct (60%)** to pass
- All possible questions are available online in advance!
